# Zoeify Music Player

A modern music player that brings your favorite music together in one place. Discover, search, download, and enjoy music from Spotify and YouTube with a seamless listening experience.

## Features

- **Music Search** – Search and discover your favorite tracks.
- **Spotify Playlist Import** – Import your Spotify playlists.
- **Spotify Support** – Play tracks using Spotify URLs.
- **YouTube Support** – Play tracks using YouTube URLs.
- **Music Downloads** – Download your favorite tracks for offline listening.
- **Seamless Playback** – Enjoy smooth and uninterrupted music playback.
- **Smart Queue Recommendations** – Get relevant track recommendations for your listening experience.
- **Queue Management** – Organize and manage your upcoming tracks.
- **Modern UI** – A clean, simple, and intuitive interface.

## Supported Sources

- Spotify Tracks
- Spotify Playlists
- YouTube Videos
- YouTube Music URLs
